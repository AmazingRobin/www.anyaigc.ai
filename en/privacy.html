Welcome to AnyAIGC (referred to below as “the Platform” or “we”), at anyaigc.ai. We take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy is meant to help you understand how we collect, use, store and share your information. By using our services, website and related products, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

1. Information we collect

To provide you with a stable service, we need to collect the following types of information:

1.1 Account information

When you register an account, we collect your:

  • Email address
  • Username / nickname
  • Password (stored encrypted)
  • Contact details (such as a mobile phone number, used for verification or urgent notices)

1.2 Usage data and logs

While you use the Platform's services, we automatically record certain technical data for billing, troubleshooting and security monitoring:

  • Metadata: request timestamps, source IP addresses, request types, usage volume, and the like
  • Content data (in transit): your input content and the model's output content
Note: except where laws and regulations require it, we do not persistently store your content data for the purpose of training models.

1.3 Payment information

We process transactions through third-party payment processors. We do not directly store your full credit card number or sensitive bank account information; we keep only transaction records, order numbers and top-up amounts for reconciliation.

2. How we use your information

  • Delivering the core service: verifying your identity, processing your requests and returning results.
  • Billing and account management: accurately calculating your usage and deducting the corresponding charges from your account balance.
  • System maintenance and security: monitoring server load, detecting abnormal traffic and handling technical faults.
  • Notices and communication: sending you notices of service changes, top-up reminders or account security alerts.
  • Legal compliance: complying with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Sharing and disclosure of information

3.1 Third-party service providers

When you use the Platform's services, the content of your requests may be forwarded to the relevant third-party service providers for processing. Those providers process your data under their own privacy policies.

3.2 Infrastructure providers

We may share necessary non-content data with third-party companies that provide hosting, database, security protection and payment services, in order to keep the Platform running.

3.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose your information in the following circumstances:

  • To comply with a court order, legal process, or a mandatory requirement of a government authority
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of AnyAIGC, our users or the public

4. Data storage and security

4.1 Data storage

Your account and metadata are stored on our secure servers. For log data, we may use professional databases for archival analysis, mainly for performance optimization and auditing.

4.2 Data security

We take industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • All traffic is transmitted with TLS/SSL encryption
  • Sensitive credentials are stored as encrypted hashes
  • Strict internal access controls are in place, so that only authorized personnel can access core systems

4.3 Data retention

We retain your personal information until you close your account. For access logs, we generally keep them for 30 days for troubleshooting, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights

  • Access and correction: log in to the console to view your usage records, balance and profile.
  • Account deletion: you may contact us to request closure of your account. After closure, we will delete your sensitive personal information (except for transaction records that the law requires us to retain).
  • Data export: you have the right to request an export of your transaction records and principal usage data.

6. Cross-border data transfers

By using the Platform's services, you understand that your data may be transferred across borders. If your request involves an overseas service, the data will be transferred to the jurisdiction where the relevant provider is located. We will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep such transfers secure.

7. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy as our business develops. For material changes, we will notify you by a notice on the website or by email. We recommend that you review this page periodically for the latest Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us as follows:
