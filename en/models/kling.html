06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Kling API models are available?

The Kling family has 14 video, audio and creative-tool models covering generation, references, avatars, audio, lip sync, subjects, effects and extension.

How do I choose a Kling model?

Start with Kling Video, Kling 3.0 Turbo or Kling Video V4 for video generation; use Motion Control for motion transfer, Avatar Image2Video for avatars, and Kling Audio for sound or speech.

What does it cost?

Models are billed per call or per second. Pricing starts at $0.00169 per call; check the model plaza for live prices.

Is concurrency limited?

Kling models have unlimited concurrency by default on AnyAIGC.

How is authentication handled?

Use Authorization: Bearer <API Key> and read the key from the ANYAIGC_API_KEY environment variable.

Can I call Kling from mainland China?

Yes. Register with AnyAIGC and call the API at https://anyaigc.ai.

Can you issue VAT special invoices?

Can enterprises pay by bank transfer and sign a contract?

Yes. Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer, sign a contract, and receive dedicated customer and technical support.

What payment methods are supported?

We support WeChat Pay, Alipay, USDT, credit cards and enterprise bank transfer.

Can unused balance be refunded?
