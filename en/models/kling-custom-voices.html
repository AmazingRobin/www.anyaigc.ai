SPECS

Specs and how to choose it

Custom Voices extracts a voice from an uploaded audio or video file or from an eligible past generation; exactly one source must be given.

Uploads can be mp3, wav, mp4 or mov, with one clean voice and no noise, 5 to 30 seconds long.

Eligible past generations are V2.6 videos with sound on, Avatar API videos and Lip Sync API videos.

Voice names are limited to 20 characters, and unused voices can be deleted through the API.

This page lists Custom voice at $0.00844/call, matching the pricing table above.

Compared with kling-audio: Custom Voices creates a reusable new voice, while Kling Audio generates speech and effects with built-in voices.

Specs come from the vendor's official documentation (klingai.com). The table on this page is the price, and the model catalog is authoritative for live rates.
