Seedance API FAQ

How much does the Seedance 2.0 Fast API cost?

AnyAIGC bills per 1M tokens, with rates varying by resolution and reference-video input. 480p / 720p (Without reference video): $4.914 / 1M tokens; 480p / 720p (With reference video): $2.896 / 1M tokens. See the model catalog for live rates.

What can Seedance take as input?

Seedance 2.0 accepts text, images, audio and video as references, with video editing, extension and native audio. 2.0 Fast prioritizes speed, 2.0 mini is designed for high-frequency workloads, and 2.5 adds stronger long narratives, references and precise edits.

How many videos does one request return?

A create request returns one task ID. After the asynchronous task finishes, query it to obtain one video URL. Duration is controlled by the duration field or the model.

Which resolutions, ratios and durations are supported?

Seedance 2.0 supports 480p, 720p, 1080p and 4K; the 4K tier is available now. Seedance 2.5 supports 480p, 720p and 1080p, with duration 4–30 or -1. Fast and mini have 480p / 720p pricing tiers. Follow each model’s API documentation for aspect ratio and duration parameters.

How do I retrieve the result?

Save the id returned by the create call, then call GET /api/v3/contents/generations/tasks/{id}. When it succeeds, read content.video_url. The exact status values come from the API response.

Is there a concurrency limit?

AnyAIGC sets no concurrency limit for Seedance, and every account gets this by default. Use a queue and retry strategy for large batches.

Can AnyAIGC issue invoices, including VAT special invoices?

Yes. AnyAIGC supports Chinese VAT general and special invoices based on the actual top-up amount.

Can a company pay by bank transfer and sign a contract?

Yes. Companies can top up by bank transfer, sign a service contract, and get a dedicated support and technical contact.

Which payment methods are supported?

Top up with WeChat Pay, Alipay, USDT or a credit card. Companies can also pay by bank transfer. Balances are in USD and work across the platform.

Is there free trial credit for new users?

Yes. New accounts get 2 million tokens of trial credit, and top-ups start at $1.

Can I get a refund on my balance?

Unused balance can be refunded with no fee. Contact support for the process.

Are these the real models? Is my data safe?

Requests go through official model channels. Unless required by law, prompts and outputs are not persistently stored or used for training; the console shows request details.
