Get started with the Grok video API

  1. Sign up for AnyAIGC. New accounts get 2M free tokens, and you can top up from $1.
  2. Create an API key in the console and save it as the ANYAIGC_API_KEY environment variable.
  3. Submit a job to POST https://anyaigc.ai/v1/videos/generations with model set to grok-imagine-video-1.5-preview, then poll GET /v1/videos/{request_id} for the result.

Video generation is asynchronous: you get a job ID back, poll until it completes, then fetch the video link. The link is temporary, so download it promptly. Field reference in the AnyAIGC docs: Create video, Get video, Edit video.

Grok Video API FAQ

How much does the Grok video API cost?

On AnyAIGC you pay per second of output video: Video 1.5 is $0.0476 (480p) and $0.0834 (720p) / sec; 1.5 Lite is $0.0119 (480p), $0.0179 (720p) and $0.0834 (1080p) / sec; Video is $0.0298 (480p) and $0.0417 (720p) / sec. Image to video adds a per-image input charge. A 6-second 1.5 Lite clip at 480p costs about $0.071. Live prices are on the model catalog.

Video 1.5, 1.5 Lite or Video: which one should I use?

Grok Imagine Video 1.5 (grok-imagine-video-1.5-preview) leads the series on visuals, motion and dialogue, from $0.0476 / sec, a good fit for ads and finished pieces. 1.5 Lite (grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite) is the cheapest in the series, from $0.0119 / sec, goes up to 1080p, and suits high-volume jobs and drafts. Grok Imagine Video (grok-imagine-video) is the first-generation model, from $0.0298 / sec, and the only one in the series that edits an uploaded video.

How long can a video be? Which resolutions and aspect ratios are supported?

duration takes 1–15 seconds and you pay for the actual length. resolution: Video 1.5 and Video support 480p and 720p; 1.5 Lite supports 480p, 720p and 1080p. aspect_ratio supports 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1; for image to video the frame follows the input image.

Why don’t I get the video back right away?

Video generation is asynchronous. Submitting returns a job ID; poll GET /v1/videos/{request_id} about every 10 seconds and read the video link once the job completes. Most jobs take tens of seconds to a few minutes. The link is temporary, so download and store the file promptly.

Do the videos have sound?

Yes. Grok Imagine generates picture and sound together, including sound effects, ambience and dialogue, so you don’t need a separate voiceover. Video 1.5 and 1.5 Lite support lip-synced speech.

Do I need an xAI account? Does it work from mainland China?

No xAI account needed. Sign up on AnyAIGC to get an API key, no overseas credit card required. The endpoint is https://anyaigc.ai, reachable directly from mainland China, and the same key works with 300+ other models on the platform.

Is there a concurrency limit?

AnyAIGC sets no concurrency limit on the Grok Video models. Every account gets this by default, so batch jobs don’t need a quota request.
