SPECS

Specs and how to choose it

Motion Control transfers motion from a reference video onto the character in a reference image, with 3.0 and 2.6 versions.

When the character follows the video's orientation the reference video can be up to 30 seconds; when it follows the image, up to 10 seconds; the minimum is 3 seconds.

The reference video can be up to 100MB with sides between 340 and 3850px, and the character image up to 50MB.

Output is 720P or 1080P, with 720P as the default.

This page lists V2.6 std 720P at $0.0844/s, V2.6 pro 1080P at $0.1350/s, V3.0 std 720P at $0.1519/s, V3.0 pro 1080P at $0.2026/s, matching the pricing table above.

Compared with kling-avatar-image2video: Motion Control drives a character with a reference video, while Avatar Image2Video drives a portrait with audio.

Specs come from the vendor's official documentation (klingai.com). The table on this page is the price, and the model catalog is authoritative for live rates.
