HappyHorse 1.0

Specs and how to choose it

happyhorse-1.0-t2v is the HappyHorse 1.0 model for text-to-video from a text prompt.

Alibaba Cloud's documentation lists 720P and 1080P output, 3 to 15 seconds per clip, 24 fps, MP4, with audio generated alongside the video.

This page lists 720P video at $0.1161 / second, 1080P video at $0.2065 / second, the same figures as the pricing table above.

happyhorse-1.1-t2v adds a 480P tier on top of 1.0. Choose 1.1 when you want low-resolution drafts.

Specs come from the Alibaba Cloud Model Studio video generation docs. The table on this page is the price, and the model catalog is authoritative for live rates.

FAQ

HappyHorse 1.0 T2V API FAQ

What is HappyHorse 1.0 T2V for?

HappyHorse 1.0 for text-to-video. Turn descriptions of scenes, characters, action, and camera movement into video.

Which resolutions and durations does happyhorse-1.0-t2v support?

Per Alibaba Cloud's documentation: 720P and 1080P, 3–15 seconds per clip, 24 fps, MP4, with audio generated.

What is the difference between HappyHorse 1.1 and 1.0?

Version 1.1 adds a 480P tier; everything else matches 1.0: 3–15 seconds, 24 fps, MP4, with audio.
