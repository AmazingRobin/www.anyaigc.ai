Python
import json
import os
import urllib.request

payload = {
    "model": "MiniMax-M3.1-Flash",
    "messages": [
        {
            "role": "user",
            "content": "Design three clear API endpoints for a to-do application."
        }
    ]
}

request = urllib.request.Request(
    "https://anyaigc.ai/v1/chat/completions",
    data=json.dumps(payload, ensure_ascii=False).encode("utf-8"),
    headers={
        "Authorization": "Bearer " + os.environ["ANYAIGC_API_KEY"],
        "Content-Type": "application/json",
    },
    method="POST",
)
with urllib.request.urlopen(request, timeout=120) as response:
    result = json.load(response)

print(json.dumps(result, ensure_ascii=False, indent=2))

FAQ

MiniMax API FAQ

Pricing, endpoints, results and enterprise access in one place.

What are the starting prices for MiniMax APIs?

M3.1 Flash input is $0.0603 per 1M tokens; Speech Turbo is $0.3176 and HD is $0.5559 per 10,000 characters; H3 768P output is $0.0750 per second. Units differ by service. See the full table and the catalog for current prices. M3.1 Flash saves 59%; M3 and M2.5 save 32% versus official pricing. Savings apply to the rates shown here; see the catalog for current offers.

How should I choose among the 12 models?

Choose M3.1 Flash, M3, M2.7 or M2.5 for chat and development; H3 for video; one of six Speech versions and HD/Turbo variants for narration; and Voice Design for text-guided voices. Test with your own workload and voice first.

Do these models share one endpoint?

They share an AnyAIGC API key and domain. Chat uses /v1/chat/completions, H3 uses /minimax/v2/video_generation, speech uses /minimax/v1/t2a_v2 or /minimax/v1/t2a_async_v2, and Voice Design uses /minimax/v1/voice_design.

How do I receive video, speech and voice-design results?

H3 creation returns a task_id first, while synchronous speech returns audio-related JSON. Voice Design has inconsistent result fields in its documentation; retain the full response and confirm the contract. Each guide includes a minimal request.

Are there concurrency or RPM limits?

AnyAIGC offers unlimited concurrency for MiniMax, with no fixed RPM cap. Generation still takes time; configure timeouts, error handling and appropriate retries in your application.

Can generated content be used commercially?

Commercial use of the models and their outputs is governed by MiniMax's official terms. Ensure you have the relevant rights to reference material, voices and generated content. AnyAIGC is an independent API provider.

Can AnyAIGC issue invoices, including VAT special invoices?

Yes. AnyAIGC can issue invoices for actual top-ups, including Chinese VAT general and VAT special invoices. Contact enterprise support to arrange them.

Can my company pay by bank transfer and sign a contract?

Yes. Companies can top up by corporate bank transfer, sign a service contract, and work with dedicated support and a technical contact for procurement and integration.

Which payment methods are supported?

You can top up with WeChat Pay, Alipay, USDT or a credit card. Companies can also pay by bank transfer and get VAT general or special invoices. Your balance is in USD and works across every model on the platform.

Is there free trial credit for new users?

Yes. New accounts get 2 million tokens of trial credit, so you can test before deciding to top up. Top-ups start at $1.

Can I get a refund on my balance?

Yes. Unused balance can be refunded with no fee. Contact support to start a refund.

Are these the real models? Is my data safe?

Requests go to official model channels, never cheaper or reverse-engineered substitutes. Unless the law requires it, we do not persistently store your prompts or outputs or use them for training, and the console shows tokens and cost for every request.
