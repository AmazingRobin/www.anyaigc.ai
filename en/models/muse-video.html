What is Muse Video?

Muse Video is the video model Meta Superintelligence Labs previewed alongside Muse Image, built on the same pretraining base. Meta says it is competitive on prompt adherence, visual fidelity and temporal consistency, and it generates audio natively: ambience, foley, voiceover and dialogue come out with the picture.

According to Meta, Muse Video ranked No. 3 on Arena for text-to-video as of July 5, 2026. It is an early preview, and Meta notes it is still closing gaps in audio-video sync and physically accurate fast motion.

On AnyAIGC the model name is muse-video. It runs as an async task on POST /v1/videos, supports text-to-video and first-frame image-to-video, and returns a 1280×720 landscape or 720×1280 portrait MP4 at $0.0397 per second.

What Muse Video can do

  • Native audio

    Foley, ambience, voiceover and dialogue are generated with the picture, so there is no separate dubbing step. In Meta's sample every dropped orange lands with a sound.

  • Prompt adherence

    Counts, order and actions follow the prompt. The sample asks for exactly five ducklings and keeps five on screen the whole time.

  • Temporal consistency

    Subjects keep the same look through the clip, even after tumbling or turning around.

  • UGC-style ads

    Handheld shake, focus shifts and a creator talking to camera, so you get a spot that looks self-shot without actors or a crew.

  • Talking characters

    Write the lines in your prompt and the character says them, with lip movement matched to the audio.

  • Camera and mood

    First-person walks and handheld follow shots track the prompt, with lighting, weather and ambient sound setting the mood.

Get started with the Muse Video API

  1. Sign up for AnyAIGC. New accounts get 2M free tokens, and top-ups start at $1.
  2. Create an API key in the console and save it as ANYAIGC_API_KEY.
  3. Submit a task to POST https://anyaigc.ai/v1/videos with model set to muse-video, then poll GET /v1/videos/{id} until status is completed.

Status goes queued → in_progress → completed / failed. Clips up to 10 seconds take about 1–2 minutes, 15–30 seconds about 3–7 minutes. On failure you get an error: video_generation_failed means no video was produced (policy or a temporary upstream error), so rephrase or retry later; video_rejected means the output failed checks and was discarded, so just retry. Muse Video API FAQ

How much does the Muse Video API cost?

On AnyAIGC, Muse Video (muse-video) costs $0.0397 per second, billed on the requested seconds: $0.199 for 5 seconds, $0.397 for 10 and $1.19 for 30. The amount is reserved on submit, failed tasks are refunded, and live pricing is in the model catalog.

What is Muse Video?

Muse Video is the video model Meta Superintelligence Labs previewed alongside Muse Image, on the same pretraining base, with native audio. It is an early preview and, per Meta, ranked No. 3 on Arena for text-to-video as of July 5, 2026.

How long can a video be?

seconds takes an integer from 4 to 30 (default 4). Output only rounds up: 4 or 5 gives 5 seconds, 7 or 8 gives 8, 11–15 gives 15, and so on. Clips over 10 seconds are generated in segments with a cut at 10 and 20 seconds. Billing uses the requested seconds.

Which sizes and resolutions are supported?

size is 1280x720 (landscape) or 720x1280 (portrait), landscape by default. resolution can only be 720p and may be omitted. Output is MP4 with audio.

Can I generate video from an image?

Yes. Pass an image as input_reference to use it as the first frame: an image URL or data URL (PNG, JPEG or WebP), or upload the file directly with multipart/form-data. File IDs are not supported.

How long does it take, and how do I get the result?

It is asynchronous: POST /v1/videos returns a task id, then poll GET /v1/videos/{id} every 10–20 seconds. Clips up to 10 seconds take about 1–2 minutes, 15–30 seconds about 3–7 minutes.

Am I charged if generation fails?

No. Failed tasks are refunded automatically, and invalid parameters return 400 (client_local_validation) without creating a task or charging you.

Is there a concurrency limit? Does it work from China?

AnyAIGC sets no concurrency limit on Muse Video for any account. The base URL is https://anyaigc.ai, reachable from mainland China, with no Meta account needed, and the same key works for 300+ other models.
